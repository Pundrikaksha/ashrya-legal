PocketKit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

PocketKit is an offline Android toolkit (flashlight, calculator, unit converter, compass, bubble level, sound meter, QR scanner/generator, magnifier). There is no account and no PocketKit backend.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, recents, theme, haptics, converter units, QR history, and Premium unlock status stay on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes them.

Camera and microphone

Camera is used only while QR scan or the magnifier is open. Frames are not saved as photos and are not uploaded by PocketKit. Microphone is used only while the sound meter is open (foreground). Audio is not saved.

Sensors

Compass and bubble level use the magnetometer and accelerometer. PocketKit does not ask for location.

Ads and purchases

The free app shows ads via Google AdMob, which may use an advertising ID until you own Premium. Premium is a one-time Google Play Billing purchase handled by Google Play. Network access is used for AdMob, consent when required, and Play Billing.
