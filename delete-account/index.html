Delete your Ashrya account

You can delete your Ashrya account from inside the app:

  1. Open Ashrya on your phone.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

That removes your Firebase sign-in, conversation history stored for that account, and favorites/streak saved on that device.

If in-app deletion fails, email support@ashrya.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will remove the account and associated cloud data.
