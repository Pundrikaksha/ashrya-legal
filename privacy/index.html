Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Ashrya is a reflective companion inspired by the Bhagavad Gita. It is not therapy, medical care, or crisis support. This policy describes the information the Android app handles.

Information we process

Account

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives the name, email address, and identifier Google shares with the app. If you continue as a guest, Firebase creates an anonymous account with no email address. We use this only to keep your conversations with your sign-in.

Conversations

Messages you write, and the replies generated for you, are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account so you can reopen them. To write a reply, the text of the conversation window (a recent portion of the thread, plus your language and length preference) is sent to Google's Gemini API. Ashrya does not sell chat content and does not use it to train Ashrya's own models. Google processes those prompts under its API terms.

Daily limit

We store a count of how many free messages you send on a given day so the free tier can reset. Ashrya Pro status is read from RevenueCat.

On this device only

Saved favorite reflections and your reflection streak stay in local app storage on the phone. They are not uploaded by Ashrya. Language, answer length, theme, and whether you finished onboarding are also stored on the device.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Google Play. The price you see is the one Google Play returns for your country. RevenueCat receives a purchase token and an app user id to unlock the Pro entitlement. Ashrya does not receive or store your card number. Pro removes ads and the daily free-message limit.

Advertising

The free tier may show Google ads (a banner, and sometimes a full-screen ad when you start a new chat). Ashrya Pro shows none. AdMob may use the advertising identifier and a consent signal. Where the law requires a choice (for example the EEA and UK), the app asks before requesting ads. In places where that form is not required, including India, ads can load without an extra dialog. Chat text is not sent to AdMob.

Fonts

The app may download font files from Google Fonts so text can render. That request does not include your conversations.

We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

Why we use it

To sign you in, keep your private conversation history, generate a reflection, enforce the free daily limit, show ads on the free tier, and restore a subscription you already bought.

How long it stays

Conversation history and the daily count remain until you delete your account or ask us to delete them. You can delete the account in Settings → Delete account. That removes the Firebase sign-in, Firestore data for that account (sessions, messages, and daily counts), and the favorites and streak stored on that device. If deletion fails, email support@ashrya.app and we will remove the account.

Uninstalling the app removes on-device favorites and streak. Conversations stored in Firestore remain until the account is deleted.

Children

Ashrya is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them.
